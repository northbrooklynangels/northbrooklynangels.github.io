Angel Babies is a biannual event in collaboration with our neighborhood partners to collect and give away all things children: clothes, gear, shoes, books, toys, etc. This massive effort gives local families a way to pass along items they no longer need to others who could use them.

Our Angel Babies giveaways are open-to-all and are on a first-come, first-served basis. Attendees are admitted in waves to browse the inventory and select items. Everything is free of charge. No pre-registration is required.

There is a limited amount of gear available (strollers, car seats, etc) and it usually goes quickly. Residents of North Brooklyn (11222, 11211, 11249, and 11206) can enroll with [Little Angels](/little-angels) for assistance with gear items year-round.

# How You Can Help

## Volunteer

Shifts are posted on our volunteer portal where you can sign up to participate. If you have questions or issues, feel free to reach out to us at [volunteer@northbrooklynangels.org](mailto:volunteer@northbrooklynangels.org).

* [**Friday, 10/23: Donation Collection & Loading**](https://volunteer.northbrooklynangels.org/shift/donation-collection-loading-a0f431)  
  Lots of help needed to empty our partner’s facility of donated items, load them into volunteer vehicles (and our truck), and unload into our event space. We need muscle for this shift! Volunteers should be able to lift bags that can be up to 40 lbs.
* [**Monday, 10/26: Sorting & Set-Up**](https://volunteer.northbrooklynangels.org/shift/donation-sorting-event-set-up-f54640)  
  Help us sort bags of donated clothing, toys, and books! You don’t have to work the full shift -- give the time you can! 
* **Tuesday, 10/27: Sorting & Set-Up ([midday shift](https://volunteer.northbrooklynangels.org/shift/donation-sorting-event-set-up-e89baa), [early evening shift](https://volunteer.northbrooklynangels.org/shift/donation-sorting-event-set-up-early-evening-shift-f03180))**  
  Help us sort bags of donated clothing, toys, and books! You don’t have to work the full shift -- give the time you can!
* [**Wednesday, 10/28: Sorting & Set-Up**](https://volunteer.northbrooklynangels.org/shift/donation-sorting-event-set-up-f50e7a)
  Help us sort bags of donated clothing, toys, and books! You don’t have to work the full shift -- give the time you can! 
* [**Thursday, 10/29: Event Support**](https://volunteer.northbrooklynangels.org/shift/event-support-da4aa0)  
  Giveaway day! Help staff tables of donated items, assist guests with finding what they need, provide directions & wayfinding to attendees, carry larger loads up the stairs, and contribute to tear down. We need lots of volunteers this day, especially Spanish speakers!

## Donate

For a couple weeks prior to a giveaway, several neighborhood partners graciously open their spaces as [donation sites](http://bit.ly/AngelBabiesMap). We accept anything related to pregnancy, infancy, and childhood. Think clothes, shoes, jackets, books, toys, etc. *Please only donate items in good condition!*
